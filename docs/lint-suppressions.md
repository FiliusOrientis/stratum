## Rule reconciliation (Biome → oxlint)

Biome disables `noVoid`. Oxlint's `typescript/no-floating-promises` (type-aware) replaces it: the oxlint rule reports every unhandled promise and treats an explicit `void` as the intent marker, while Biome's `noVoid` only banned the operator. Coverage does not decrease.
